What to write, in order

  1. Apple Music ingestwrite

    Promote artists.py into a repeatable export: artist, play count, loved and favorited. Resolve the names to QIDs through the existing seed tool, and keep the data on your machine. Issue #350.

  2. Interest modelwrite

    Blend your explicit ratings with the Apple Music signals into one confidence per entity, with the rating winning where you gave one. Likely amends ADRs 33 and 48. The evaluation harness can check it against ratings you already have. Issue #351.

  3. A seventh MCP tool for what to explore nextextend

    Needs a new ADR superseding the "no seventh tool" decisions (26 and 45). Return candidates highest confidence first, each with its cited routes. Ratings stay readable by Claude; notes still never reach it. Issue #352.

  4. The guided loopwrite

    The user-guide section and prompt that make Claude ask for the next candidates, expand one, explain it by its routes, and offer to record your rating. This is what turns the tools into the daily habit. Issue #353.

  5. setlist-scout wiringwire up

    Today it reads a hand-kept seed list. Segue writes it a names-only list of the artists you rate highly, one way. Showing its listings as a reason to explore a band comes later. Issue #354.

Entity resolution is marked "extend" because the seed tool already resolves lists by title and kind, and Apple Music ingest will lean on it. Whether it copes with Apple Music's artist spellings is untested.
